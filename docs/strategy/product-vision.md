# Product Vision

## Mission

Make a coffee order feel like unboxing a premium product — where the craft of the site matches the craft claimed by the coffee itself. The site is the first taste.

## Experience pillars

- **Sensory craft over information density.** Every screen leads with how the product looks/feels/moves before it leads with copy or price.
- **Premium restraint.** Minimal, confident, unhurried — glassmorphism, glow, and motion are used with intent, never as decoration for its own sake.
- **Performance as craft.** A beautiful interaction that drops frames is a broken interaction. 60fps and sub-second interactivity are treated as design requirements, not engineering afterthoughts.
- **Accessible luxury.** Premium and accessible are not in tension here — reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader support are built into the experience, not a degraded fallback bolted on later.
- **Earn the interaction.** Nothing animates because it can; every motion communicates state, hierarchy, or delight the user will actually notice.
