# Personas

## The Discerning Regular

Orders the same complex drink every day and knows exactly how it should look and taste. Goal: reorder fast, but wants confidence the order is exact — right milk, right shot count, right size. Frustrated by generic coffee sites that treat every drink as one SKU with a size dropdown. This site must nail: fast, precise customization that visually confirms the exact drink before ordering (Milestone 4+).

## The Gift / Occasion Buyer

Buying for someone else, or treating themselves for a specific moment (a gift card, a first visit, a seasonal drink). Doesn't know the menu well. Frustrated by dense menus with no sense of what anything actually looks/tastes like. This site must nail: the hero/menu experience communicating mood and craft visually, and an AI-assisted recommendation path that doesn't require domain knowledge (Milestone 7).

## The Design-Curious Browser

Isn't necessarily buying today — arrived because the site itself was recommended or shared for its craft (Awwwards-adjacent audience, other designers/engineers). Goal: be impressed, poke at every interaction, check performance and accessibility as much as visuals. Frustrated by sites that look impressive in a screenshot but feel janky or slow in the hand. This site must nail: the hero's first-load impression, 60fps interaction, and every micro-interaction rewarding closer inspection rather than falling apart under it.
