# Competitive & Inspiration Notes

## Premium DTC coffee brand sites (e.g. Blue Bottle, Onyx Coffee Lab tier)

**Emulate**: confident product photography, restrained typography, a real sense of material/craft in the imagery.
**Avoid**: mostly static — scroll-triggered fades at best, no real interactivity. We're building an experience, not a catalog with parallax.

## Awwwards-tier product sites (e.g. Apple product pages, Stripe, Linear-style marketing sites)

**Emulate**: scroll-driven storytelling with genuine narrative pacing, restrained color/type systems that still feel premium, motion that clarifies state rather than decorating it.
**Avoid**: over-animating every element such that nothing reads as more important than anything else — hierarchy gets lost.

## 3D configurator references (sneaker/watch/car customizers)

**Emulate**: the live-3D-updates-instantly-on-selection pattern (Milestone 4's target), camera presets that frame each decision clearly, exploded/assembly views that teach the product's construction.
**Avoid**: configurators that feel like a technical demo bolted onto a page — ours must feel like part of one continuous experience (hero → customize → order), not a separate tool.
